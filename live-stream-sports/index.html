How it works

The XbotGo is an AI tracking camera built for sports. I mount it at rink side, and it follows the play on its own — panning and zooming with the puck like a cameraman who never gets tired, never misses a rush, and never has to choose which kid to follow.

  • AI auto-tracking follows the play — no camera operator needed
  • Streams live to YouTube so grandparents and family can watch from anywhere
  • Scoreboard on screen, so viewers always know the score
  • The full game stays online to rewatch, share and download highlights from
