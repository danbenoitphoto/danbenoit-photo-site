About

Dan Benoit Photo — Embrun, Ontario, since 2006.

Dan Benoit Photo was founded in 2006 out of my desire to create a company through which I could explore my passion for photography and capturing precious moments in the lives of others.

“Capturing precious moments in the lives of others.”

From engagement sessions to wedding photography, newborn and maternity sessions and more, Dan Benoit Photo artistically caters to all of your photography needs.

We take great pride in making sure that your special day is remembered forever and that every single moment is captured.

Today, from Embrun, Ontario, I also shoot real estate and drone photo/video work for realtors and businesses across Eastern Ontario — see the Drone & Real Estate page.
